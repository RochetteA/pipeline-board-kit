03 / GET YOUR COPY

Make your copy & go

The whole board, engine included, lives in a ready-made template. You don't install anything & you never touch code.

Bringing your leads with you? A walkthrough for auto-importing from an instant-form tab & carrying in your old Trello list is on its way as its own guide. The board works fully by hand from minute one either way.

Rather have the board inside the KPI tracker sheet you already use instead of a fresh copy? Skip this section & follow Add it to your existing KPI sheet.

  1. Open the Pipeline Board Kit template. Google shows a Make a copy button. Click it.
  2. That's the install. The sheet that opens is yours: the board is already built, the engine is already inside, & nothing in it reports back to anyone. Rename it whatever you like.
  3. Add your people on the Pipeline tab & start moving them with the Stage dropdown. The Form Q1 to Q4 columns hold instant-form answers; every form asks different questions, so type your own questions right over those four headers.
  4. Want the daily "who's due" email? Pipeline board menu → 3. Turn ON automation, then approve Google's permission popup. It may say "Google hasn't verified this app"; that's your own copy's script asking. Click Advanced → Go to → Allow. (More on that popup just below.)

About the authorization popup

The first time you run the menu, Google asks you to authorize the script. You may see a screen that says "Google hasn't verified this app." That warning shows up for every personal script anyone writes in their own sheet. It's yours, running in your account, touching only this spreadsheet & the reminder email it sends you.

Click Advanced, then Go to (your project name), then Allow. That's it, & you only do it once.

04 / EXISTING SHEET

Add it to your existing KPI sheet

Already have a KPI sheet your leads flow into, & want the board living right beside them instead of in a fresh copy? Copy the tabs over; there's no code in this either.

The board itself (columns, colors, the Stage dropdown, the DUE flags & the ⚠) is all formulas & formatting, so the two tabs can move into your tracker like any other sheets:

  1. Make your copy of the template first, if you haven't already.
  2. In your copy, right-click the Pipeline tab at the bottom → Copy to → Existing spreadsheet → pick your KPI tracker.
  3. Do the same for the Pipeline Board tab.
  4. Open your KPI tracker & rename the two new tabs: "Copy of Pipeline" becomes Pipeline, "Copy of Pipeline Board" becomes Pipeline Board. The renames matter; they're how the board's formulas find each other, & any #REF! errors vanish once both names are right.

What the tab-copy doesn't bring: the engine. Google attaches scripts to a spreadsheet, not to its tabs, so the self-stamping dates, the checkbox sending, the instant-form auto-import & the morning email stay behind. You'd move stages with the dropdown & type Last Touch dates yourself; the DUE flags & ⚠ still work off whatever dates you enter. Want the automation in your tracker too? That setup is coming as its own walkthrough.

05 / EVERY DAY

The daily rhythm

  • Moving a card is one dropdown. On the Pipeline tab, change the person's Stage. The board redraws instantly. Never edit the board tab itself; it politely warns you if you try.
  • Dates handle themselves. Changing a stage stamps Last Touch to today & Next FU to 2 days out. Booked a call for Thursday? Type Thursday's date into Next FU. A date you set always wins.
  • DUE & the ⚠. When someone's follow-up day arrives, or they've sat untouched 2+ days, their row flags DUE & their card gets a ⚠. Resting stages like My Team & Leads Archive show a dash instead, so finished people never nag you.
  • New people arrive on their own. With automation on, form leads land at the first stage with their answers filled in. You can also tick the → Pipeline checkbox on a lead tab to send someone over yourself.
  • The morning email. Around 8 AM, any day someone is due, you get the list: who, what stage, your notes. Open the sheet, work the list, close the app.
